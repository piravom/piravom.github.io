# Piravom
Town Portal for the town Piravom
Piravom is a municipal town in Ernakulam district, Kerala, India, situated
on the banks of the Muvattupuzha River. 
This portal is a comprehensive directory of local
businesses, services, restaurants, attractions, events, houses of worship, emergency contacts, and
transport information for Piravom and the surrounding area.
