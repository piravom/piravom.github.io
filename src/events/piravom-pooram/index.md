The Piravom Pooram is a dazzling spectacle of Kerala's temple festival tradition. Ornately decorated elephants line up in procession, while traditional instruments like chenda, madhalam, and kombu create a rhythmic symphony. The evening culminates in a breathtaking fireworks display that lights up the sky over Piravom.
