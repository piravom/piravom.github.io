We're thrilled to launch the **Piravom Town Blog** — a dedicated space to share news, stories, heritage pieces, and community updates from our beloved town on the banks of the Muvattupuzha River.

## What to Expect

This blog will cover a wide range of topics:

- **🏛️ Heritage & History** — Stories about Piravom's rich cultural past, from the Pazhoor Padippura to the ancient temples and churches that dot the landscape.
- **🍽️ Food & Culture** — Spotlights on local restaurants, traditional Kerala cuisine, and the culinary gems that make Piravom special.
- **🏔️ Travel & Tourism** — Guides to nearby attractions like Areekkal Waterfalls, Mayiladum Para, and the scenic river viewpoints.
- **📢 Community Updates** — Infrastructure projects, local events, town council announcements, and more.
- **💼 Business Spotlight** — Features on the shops, services, and entrepreneurs that keep our town thriving.

## A Town of Stories

Piravom has always been more than just a dot on the map. Nestled between the lush hills of the High Ranges and the backwaters of Kerala, it's a town where tradition meets progress. From the rhythmic beats of the Piravom Pooram to the quiet beauty of the Muvattupuzha at sunset — every corner has a story.

This blog is our way of telling those stories.

We invite you to explore, share your thoughts, and contribute. Whether you're a long-time resident or a first-time visitor, there's always something new to discover.

Stay tuned for more posts!
