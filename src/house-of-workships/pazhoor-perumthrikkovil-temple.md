---
name: Pazhoor Perumthrikkovil Temple
type: temple
description: An ancient 12th-century Shiva temple and protected monument by the Kerala Archaeology Department. Known for the unique phenomenon where the Muvattupuzha river changes its course upon reaching the temple. Features a square ardhamandapa with beautiful wooden carvings on the ceiling, believed to be the work of the legendary Perumthachan.
distance: ~2 km
