description: Also known as Piravom Valiyapally, one of the oldest churches in Kerala founded in the 6th century. Known as the 'Church of the Kings' (Rajakkalude Pally). Features a perpetually burning lamp that has remained lit throughout day and night for centuries. A major pilgrimage centre for Syrian Christians in Kerala, situated on a scenic hilltop on the eastern bank of the Muvattupuzha river.
