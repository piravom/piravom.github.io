Piravom has a well-established post office network to serve residents and businesses.

## Main Post Office

**Piravom Post Office** (Head Office)

- **PIN Code:** 686664
- **Location:** Near the Municipal Office, Piravom town centre
- **Phone:** 0485-2240332

**Services available:**

- Ordinary mail and registered post
- Speed Post
- Money Orders (domestic and international)
- Savings Bank accounts
- Recurring Deposit (RD) and Time Deposit (TD) accounts
- Aadhaar enrolment and update
- Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- Postal Life Insurance
- Sale of stamps and stationery
