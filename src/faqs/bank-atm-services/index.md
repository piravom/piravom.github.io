Piravom is well-served by both nationalised and private banks.

## Banks with Branches in Piravom

| Bank                                 | Location                           |
| ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| **State Bank of India (SBI)**        | Near Municipal Office, Town Centre |
| **Federal Bank**                     | Main Road, Piravom                 |
| **South Indian Bank**                | Market Road                        |
| **Kerala Gramin Bank**               | Near Bus Stand                     |
| **Piravom Cooperative Bank**         | Town Hall Road                     |
| **Piravom Service Cooperative Bank** | Market Junction                    |

## ATM Availability

- **24-hour ATMs** are available at SBI, Federal Bank, and South Indian Bank branches
- Most ATMs support all major networks (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, UPI)
- Additional standalone ATMs are located near the bus stand and market area

## Banking Hours

Standard banking hours: **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM** (Monday to Friday), **10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** (Saturday). Closed on Sundays and public holidays. ATMs operate 24/7.

## Digital Payments

Most merchants, shops, and restaurants in Piravom accept **UPI payments** (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). Card payments are accepted at larger establishments.
